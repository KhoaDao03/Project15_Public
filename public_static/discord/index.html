Bare UP/DOWN or IN UP/DOWN starts a $20 plan. IN UP $20 and IN DOWN $20 are accepted; extra commentary is not. A higher same-direction amount tops up to that total; equal or lower amounts add nothing. ADD 15 or ADDED $26 adds that amount to an active plan; ambiguous commands or multiple numbers are skipped. A dollar-only message follows the channel’s current direction. Opposite direction starts a new plan. Plans reset at :00, :15, :30 and :45. Signals use the original message time; missing timestamps are not actionable. Expired plans are reset, not confirmed closed. Plans are reconstructed from retained messages, not actual positions. All recognized cashout commands, including CASH HALF and CASHING HALF, close the entire plan. Only explicit CASH, CASH OUT, CASH OUT EVERYTHING, CASH NOW, or CLOSE ALL commands mean close all; advice does not. A trailing NOW CASH command is also recognized after a conversational lead-in; negated or conditional wording is skipped. Initial entries and explicit ADD/ADDED commands plan market buys. Later increases to the target total plan limit buys at the latest confirmed fill price unless an explicit ENTRY 55% message sets a new buy limit. Both channels use the shared gateway. First accepted entry owns each interval; other-channel calls are ignored. Each confirmed buy fill, including partial fills and additions, is planned to immediately request an 85¢ limit sell for the newly filled quantity. Duplicate fill events must not create duplicate sells. Execution status is shown per message, and confirmed fills appear in Orders & trades. Retained messages from before activation are never executed. Inferred authors use the preceding known author in the same channel and may be incorrect. Loaded messages only; missing messages and deletions may not be captured. Revisions include metadata changes, not just edits. Attachments are links, not archived files.
